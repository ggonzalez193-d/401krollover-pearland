Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 401(k) Rollover Pearland (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website, call the phone number listed on it, or submit the contact form.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide, such as your first and last name, email address, phone number, and any details you share about your situation (for example, a layoff, a job change, or an inherited account). If you call the phone number listed on this site, standard call information (such as the number you called from and the time of the call) may be recorded by our phone service provider.

Like most websites, this site may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, general device information, and the pages you visit, typically through cookies or similar technologies. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it working properly.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your questions and, if you'd like, connect you with a local independent advisor for a further conversation about your options. We do not use your information to make any decisions about your retirement account on your behalf. We may also use technical site information to improve this website and understand which pages and areas are most useful to visitors.

Phone and Text Communications

By submitting our contact form, or providing your phone number, you consent to be contacted by 401(k) Rollover Pearland and, where applicable, the advisor we connect you with, by phone, text message, or email regarding your inquiry — including by automated dialing or messaging technology. Consent to be contacted is never a condition of receiving any information or service from us. Message and data rates may apply to text communications. You can ask to stop receiving calls or texts at any time by replying STOP to a text or telling us directly during a call.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use standard website cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall site traffic and usage patterns, and may use advertising pixels (such as the Meta/Facebook Pixel) if we run online ads, to measure how well those ads perform. These tools collect general, aggregated usage information and do not by themselves identify you personally. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended if you do.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. For any single inquiry, we share your information with only one local advisor — we do not distribute your information to multiple companies. We may share the information you submit with:

  • The one local independent advisor we connect you with, so they can follow up about your inquiry directly.
  • Service providers who help us operate this website and process form submissions, such as our website hosting provider and our contact-form processing service, solely to help us deliver your message to us.
  • Anyone else, only if required to do so by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not earn a commission or any share of proceeds from any product you may or may not purchase from that advisor. See our Referral Marketing Disclosure for details on how we're compensated.

Your Choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or ask what information we have on file for you, by emailing info@401krolloverpearland.com or calling (832) 536-8693. We will respond to reasonable requests promptly.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We use reasonable measures to help protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@401krolloverpearland.com or (832) 536-8693.
