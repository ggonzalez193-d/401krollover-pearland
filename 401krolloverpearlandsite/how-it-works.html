How It Works

There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy on this site. It's built around one straightforward idea: understand your options first, decide later.

1. Read about your situation

Start with the page that matches what brought you here — a layoff, a job change, or an inherited account — and get the general picture.

2. Ask questions, if you want to

If something isn't clear, or you want to talk through your specific numbers, call (832) 536-8693 for a free, no-pressure conversation with a licensed advisor.

3. Decide on your own timeline

Nobody on this site is pushing you toward a rollover, a specific product, or a specific provider. What you do with your account, and when, is entirely up to you.
