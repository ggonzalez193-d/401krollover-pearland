Frequently Asked Questions

Are you going to tell me to roll over my 401(k)?

No. This site is educational only, and we don't recommend any specific action for your account. If you want personalized guidance, you can talk to a licensed advisor by phone, but the decision is always yours.

Do you manage money or sell investment products through this website?

No. This website doesn't manage money, sell products, or process any account transactions. It explains general options; any actual decisions and paperwork happen directly with a licensed professional, off this site.

Is there a cost to call and ask questions?

No. A phone conversation is free and comes with no obligation to do anything afterward.

What happens if I just leave my old 401(k) where it is?

Often nothing changes, and the account keeps growing or shrinking with its investments as before — but some plans automatically move out small balances, so it's worth checking your specific plan's rules.

I inherited an account — is this the same as a regular rollover?

No. Inherited accounts follow a separate set of rules that depend on your relationship to the original owner and other specifics. See our inherited account page for the general picture, and talk to an advisor for anything specific to your situation.

Do you work with one specific company or provider?

We work with a local independent advisor in the Pearland area. They can explain their own background, licensing, and how they work directly with you.
